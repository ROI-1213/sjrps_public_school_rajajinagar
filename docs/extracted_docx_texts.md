The Beginning of our Rajajinagar campus
Rooted in a rich legacy of education, the Rajajinagar campus began its journey in 1951 with a simple yet powerful vision—to provide quality education and lay a strong foundation for the generations to come.
The Society, which had been functioning as a collegiate institution since 1945, ventured into school education in May 1981 by establishing a nursery and primary school in a temporary shed at the S.J.R. Law College Hostel area. The school began with L.K.G. and 1st Standard.
The institution grew steadily, with U.K.G. and II Standard introduced in 1982–83, followed by the addition of one higher standard each year from 1983–84. The introduction of X Standard in 1990–91 marked the completion of the school’s primary and high school sections.
A significant milestone was reached in 2022–23, when SJR Primary and High School transitioned from the State Board to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and was renamed SJR Public School. The school adopted the NCERT-based curriculum, with an emphasis on academic excellence, skill development and holistic learning.
Today, SJR Public School Rajajinagar, continues this legacy by combining its rich heritage with a progressive approach to education, nurturing confident, responsible and future-ready learners.
